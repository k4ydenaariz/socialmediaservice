Frequently asked questions

Need to Know

Can you guarantee my account will be recovered?

No. Account decisions are made by the relevant platform. We provide independent guidance and help with official recovery or appeal steps, but cannot guarantee a result.

Do I need to provide my password or OTP?

No. Never share passwords, OTPs, backup codes or authentication credentials. Be cautious of anyone who asks for them.

How do I start?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the platform name, issue type and a short explanation. Do not send sensitive login details.

Are you officially connected to Meta or TikTok?

No. Zen Account Service is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, Instagram or Facebook/Meta.

Can every restriction be appealed?

Not necessarily. Appeal options depend on the platform, account status, region and reason for the restriction.

How much does the service cost?

Contact us for a case review and service quotation. Any applicable fee should be explained and agreed before work begins.
